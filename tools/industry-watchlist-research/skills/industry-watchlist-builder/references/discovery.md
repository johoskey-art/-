# Discovery: sources → roles → missing companies

## Adaptive research path

1. Retrieve latest existing master/scope and most relevant recent industry reports. Do not exhaustively crawl irrelevant files.
2. Add international industry research, trade bodies, market leaders' investor presentations, 10-K/20-F/annual reports, earnings transcripts, and official product catalogs. If no domestic sell-side coverage, start with global company and industry primary sources.
3. Translate each nontrivial chart/claim into a testable question: Who captures margin? Which product spec shifts? Does growth reflect end-user unit demand or content/pre-build/inventory? Who supplies substitutes? What actually constrains yield/capacity?
4. Sketch *physical/economic* role map independently of known stocks: INPUT → PRODUCT → SYSTEM/INTEGRATOR → CUSTOMER, adding side branches and alternative solutions as appropriate.
5. Coverage audit: investigate missing roles with role-led queries (e.g., BTM onsite generation+storage+power management integrator); prioritize major buying decisions and unavoidable bottlenecks over exhaustive market census.
6. Add candidates from competitive disclosures, supply chain/channel sources, customers' IR, supplier lists, trade publications. Avoid relying solely on search popularity or a single brokerage report.
7. Record why a discovered firm matters: CORE, STRUCTURAL, OPTION, or SIGNAL **for each product**, not globally.

## End conditions

Stop searching a role when key direct leaders/peers and a meaningful substitute/competitive risk are identified and new sources no longer change peer, economics or current EVENT. If still uncertain, keep role gap OPEN and mention it. Treat nonpublic firms as background rather than fabricated tradable equities.

## Deliver

Role map (including empty roles), company candidates (with discovery source), excluded scope, key open questions. Do not directly print an HTS list at this stage.
