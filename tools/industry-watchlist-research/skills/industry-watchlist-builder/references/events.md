# Economics-linked EVENT engine v0.3

EVENT discovery starts with `references/report-corpus.md` and `references/event-research.md`, *not* solely with a recent headline or the firms already in HOME. Load prior canonical event registry; investigate current, historical, multi-broker and cross-sector catalysts first. Preserve old non-repeated catalysts and exclude stale/superseded ones with explanation.

Use common event classes: DEMAND, SPEC-UP, BOTTLENECK, PRICE/MIX, SHARE/QUAL, CAPA/RAMP, POLICY/STRUCTURE.

An EVENT record includes canonical `event_id` and source provenance, first occurrence, updated status, legal/technical scope where relevant, affected company×product and cross-sector ties, scenario stages, Q/P/MIX/SHARE/UTIL/COST/YIELD pathway to EPS, next observable indicator, confirming and contradictory evidence. Distinguish sourced fact, brokerage interpretation, and original model inference. Do not replace uncertainty with confident prose.

The market can observe supply/contract indicators before final demand announcements. Record both **physical causality order** and **observable signal order**. Direct customers are not assumed unless a filing or reliable source supports them. A regulatory narrowing of one product may boost compliant substitutes, distributors or integrators and may also delay project buildouts. Keep direct and indirect economic effects separate.

If important policy ID, order scope, first occurrence or cross-industry review remains unverified, status=HOLD; it cannot be called a fully researched EVENT. No formula can automatically infer that every investor candidate benefits. Actual revenue attribution and business operating leverage are essential to EPS sensitivity claims.

## 중간 뉴스 검증 단계 (v0.6)

이벤트 원장의 정책/산업 주장 확정 후, **수혜기업 매핑 전에** `news-audit.md`의 PRE_BENEFICIARY 뉴스 검증을 실시한다. 언론 보도에서 계약·공장 가동·규제 적용·지연·대체재 반론을 탐색하고 원문을 확인한 뒤, EVENT→제품→기업 수익경로를 수정한다. **마지막 출력 직전** PRE_RELEASE 변경점 점검을 반복한다. ACTIVE/WATCH 주요 이벤트의 기록 누락은 `NEWS_AUDIT_HOLD`로 남긴다. 과거/최근/뉴스 주장은 독립 근거가 서로 다른 것이며, 동일 통신사 전재 기사 여러 개를 독립 확인으로 세지 않는다.
