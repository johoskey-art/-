# Company-first product roles and order

A HOME block represents a sellable product/service at a real point in the industry. It is **not** a theme, material attribute, process methodology, geographic market or event label. Some materials are products in their upstream producer's HOME; do not confuse them with the downstream assembly form.

Group priority:
1. same sellable product and similar customer class;
2. comparable Q/P/MIX/SHARE/UTIL and margin exposure;
3. direct competition/substitution or market-pricing correlation, with deviations flagged.

Split only if economic difference is material (margin, ASP, growing customer base, technical qualification, scarcity, or valuation) and useful on a trading screen. Keep 2–6 firms if natural, permit 1. The business line, not the company as a whole, is the allocation unit. Duplicate across distinct material HOME and EVENT with a recorded reason.

## Value-chain display order

1. Groups: actual material/product flows (upstream → downstream) with branches where needed, not report order nor discovered-firm order.
2. Within a group: global reference and relevant pure-play/leader → close alternatives → Korean peers → newcomers/options. Reorder only for a clearly better financial/structural reason and log it.
3. Block style: company lines, blank line, `↓ PRODUCT NAME`, first appearance one easy definition. Later appearances abbreviated. Avoid separate glossary repeating in watchlist.
4. A company that is a mixed conglomerate should be tagged to the **specific** products in the map. Do not present all group revenue as if it belongs to that product.
5. Signal companies should be labeled as signals rather than direct same-product rivals.
