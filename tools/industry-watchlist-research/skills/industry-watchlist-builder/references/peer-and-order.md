# Product-role peer formation, sensitivity and ordering — v0.2

Build actual product HOME groups, not broad AI/green/robotics themes. Preserve existing value-chain ordering unless new primary evidence changes it. In each group prefer global leaders and relevant pure plays, then near direct peers, smaller specialists, Korean competitors, recent entrants; do not force national ordering over product comparability.

Company presence never implies equal operating economics:

1. Map **company × product × role**. Evaluate `MANUFACTURER`, `SUPPLIER`, `DISTRIBUTOR`, `INTEGRATOR`, `INSTALLER`, `SERVICE` and current revenue/contract/pilot status separately. A firm can be primary HOME in cables and a valuable secondary exposure to a busway contract without claiming busway manufacturing.
2. Compare closest peers using sellable product, comparable customer type, Q/P/MIX/SHARE/UTIL/COST/YIELD, margin, qualification, scarcity and valuation; note differentiated channel/integrator exposures explicitly.
3. **Reverse audit every discovered company**: inspect actual subsidiaries, IR segment breakdown, official product categories, sales contracts and customer-facing brands for economically significant *other products*. Either add supported duplicate HOME, or document `HOLD`/`EXCLUDED` reason in the candidate log.
4. Search **small/mid-cap specialists separately** from headline incumbents. Do not use size as a rejection criterion. Stock market beta and earnings sensitivity must be separately sourced/calculated; never use an unsupported score to imply precision.
5. Exclude very weak thematic options from CORE but retain evidence-backed SIGNAL/OPTION only where they explain the industry or event. A company need not be in every product to which it has a marketing connection.

**Display:** firms (usually 2–6, permit more if economically justified), blank line, `↓ PRODUCT / ROLE`, optional one-line definition. HOME value-chain order differs from EVENT profit-transmission order. Record ordering and duplicate-placement rationale so subsequent UPDATE does not reshuffle previously checked peers.
